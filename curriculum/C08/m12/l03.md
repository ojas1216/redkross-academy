## Case 1: the payments workstation

You have a high-priority case on fictional workstation FIN-21. The intake identified an unusual process, a document request, subsequent DNS records and an outbound transfer. The second batch confirmed a specific protective control. Your assignment is to write a case chronology, assess what remains at risk and prepare an evidence-based handoff for the incident lead. Work only from the fictional capstone records. The exercise requires no connection to a service and no file execution.

The case question is intentionally narrower than ?find the attacker.? Determine whether the evidence supports an endpoint response, what the response record confirms, and what remains unknown about delivery, data and business impact. A high-confidence assessment about activity on one workstation can coexist with incomplete confidence about its cause or scope. Use the operating context and the actual observations for your recommendation.

## Establish the chronology

Use occurrence time rather than worksheet row order. A02 is a proxy request from FIN-21 at 08:01 UTC for statement.docm, observed at the recorded destination. A01 is an endpoint process relationship at 08:03, with source identity E-301. Preserve A06 as a delivery retry associated with the same observation. A03 and B27 are distinct DNS events on this host at 08:04 and 08:06. B27 arrived in the second batch. A15 shows an 8,000,000-byte transfer at 08:18. The original bytes and exact payload are unavailable throughout the exercise.

Write source labels next to the events. The proxy can say the device requested a file and later transferred a specified byte volume. The endpoint record can describe the parent and child processes. DNS records give queries, not every application action after them. A clock relationship can help correlate the events, and small differences in record time are possible, but the common workstation identity and meaningful sequence provide a better basis for considering them together than order alone. If a source had used an unverified nickname instead of an asset ID, confidence about entity linkage would need separately checking.

The case has two separate observations about state change. B20 records acceptance of an isolation request at 08:27 and explicitly says it was pending application. B21 shows that the policy applied to the verified device ID at 08:29. Preserve both in the timeline and link to the approval and control owner's verification process in the written decision. Detail what scope that record supports. A verified endpoint state does not verify identity access, mail delivery or financial recovery. The exercise provides no later evidence demonstrating that all affected systems are known or that data was recovered.

## Evaluate the scope and confidence

Begin the assessment with direct observations. A word processor started an unexpected script interpreter on a payments workstation. A document request preceded that process event and was attributed to the same device. DNS and proxy records show related destination activity in the same window. These observations support a high-priority endpoint incident review. A careful assessment can say they are consistent with suspicious document-related activity. It should explain that direct evidence of the document's content and exact causal link is not available. Preserve the strongest evidence next to the assumption that connects it.

B30 says a scoped proxy review found no second host for the three named domains in available data from 08:00 through 08:32. Without trusted coverage of other devices and channels, that is a negative result within a specific search. It supports an initial scope statement, not a guarantee that the activity is limited to one device. WKS-05 has delayed endpoint telemetry, and B29's unknown host key is not attributable from the supplied records. Neither automatically expands confirmed scope. They do affect what the team can responsibly rule out. Give both issues a follow-up owner and note which source could change the result.

Look at potential data impact on its own. A15 establishes an outbound byte count and destination for FIN-21. Different legitimate and harmful transfers may produce a similar volume. The volume alone is insufficient to say which files, records or people were affected. Request available authorised logs or independent evidence that could determine the content and business context. Finance's B22 status is a review in progress. It does not report a verified change to payment instructions. Preserve a statement of what has been requested and which owner can confirm it.

The embedded statement from the service desk is weaker than an original mail record. A09 includes a screenshot and a claim of no action by its reporter. A10 is a gateway preview. Neither proves mail delivery to FIN-21's user, or a confirmed human interaction. A mail reviewer can obtain the original where available and check recipient disposition and relevant message identifiers. If it later matches the document or destination, record the linkage with a source rather than assuming the screenshot was the actual delivery event. If it turns out unrelated, the endpoint finding still stands.

## Record action and recovery conditions

Write the protective decision as a two-part narrative. The incident lead authorised a scoped response under the simulation, and the endpoint management output is the observable evidence that policy applied to FIN-21. The request time supports an execution interval but does not replace evidence of effective state. Record containment as verified for that device and leave business and evidence tasks open. If an operator needs to restore service, preservation, verification, policy and approval determine how that proceeds. Ticket age alone should not trigger restoration.

Identify the actions the scenario does not verify. It provides no verified password or session response for the FIN-21 employee, no completed mail purge and no payment recovery. Including those actions as completed would inflate the report. It is appropriate to recommend follow-up with the proper owners and to distinguish proposed, pending and verified states. For example, a mail team can first identify confirmed matching deliveries with a trusted search and preserve evidence. Only then can the lead assess whether a scoped mail action is justified.

Consider customer and employee impact. FIN-21 is used for payment review. The owning team can arrange a clean alternative work path through approved services, and Finance can independently evaluate transactions. Do not ask the affected user to repeat the original interaction to reproduce the case. Verify availability through a safe method and route employee communication through the recognised support channel. Record both the operational workaround and its owner as part of recovery planning. This makes the case useful to management beyond the device's technical status.

## Review decisions under alternative facts

First, remove B21 from the available evidence. The supported device state reverts to pending application. The next owner should verify actual control status, not reactivate or reissue blind actions. Keep the reasons for the containment recommendation and the observed request. Without verification, do not claim that the protection was effective. Re-run your shift briefing using a cutoff just after B20 and compare it with the complete case. Only the control state and next verification should change. The observed process relationship and unresolved data question remain.

Second, imagine an independently authorised record later identifies the transfer as an approved business operation. Preserve the existing volume evidence and revise the impact assessment. That does not automatically explain the earlier process event, so record which part of the case was weakened and which part remains relevant. Ask what would independently substantiate the file and process relationship. A good assessment can incorporate benign evidence without collapsing into either dismissal or unsupported certainty.

Third, imagine another system appears in a later proxy review. Add the new source, time and entity and place its effect on scope under review. The earlier B30 finding remains true for the earlier dataset and window. An additional matching host needs independent context. You may need another containment recommendation if the lead confirms a meaningful related event, but the earlier approval for FIN-21 does not cover the new system. This variation makes the difference between an expanding hypothesis and a broader authorised operation concrete.

## Prepare the handoff

Create an executive paragraph identifying the affected business function, serious evidence, verified control state and significant unresolved impact. Keep raw event contents and any employee-specific details out of that paragraph unless authorised. Provide a technical attachment or restricted worksheet reference for the timeline, selected source IDs, bounded proxy search and each case task. Do not mix the case's endpoint activity with the suspicious VPN success solely because both occurred during the same shift. The identity decision can carry urgency without unverified linkage.

Give the endpoint owner a specific verification or recovery task, the mail owner a preserved original or delivery request, and Finance a transaction review. Give the notification owner reliable times and relevant facts through the approved channel if requested. B23 indicates a request for that handoff, not an external notice. Maintain before-and-after state in the case record for the lead. A new reviewer should find when the plan was made, when it was requested, when its effect was observed, and which ownership decisions still need action.

## Acceptance checklist

The case is ready for simulated handoff when your worksheet identifies the correct device and supporting records, distinguishes the file interpretation from its missing contents, and records the 08:29 effective state separately from 08:27 acceptance. Include the limits of B30 and at least two owned follow-up tasks. Ask a peer to identify one fact they can verify, one inference they can challenge and one decision they can take. If they cannot find all three in the handoff, improve the wording or evidence links. Accurate uncertainty is part of the grade.

## Audit the case from a second role

Act as a reviewer who receives the executive note without the detailed timeline. Underline every verb that asserts a completed event. Ask what source supports ?requested,? ?isolated,? ?transferred,? ?confirmed? or ?notified.? For this case, the management service supports applied isolation, the proxy supports the transfer volume, and the lead's coordination note supports a requested notification handoff. These words have different meanings. Replacing one with another can change who thinks they need to act. Preserve an evidence reference for each verb that has operational significance.

Review the analytical language next. An unexpected process relationship is recorded, and the file association is assessed from the timing and common device. If both appear in one sentence, identify which clause is observation and which interpretation. A good case can say ?recorded a process relationship shortly after the file request; investigating whether the document caused it.? If a later authorised file review corroborates the link, record the new basis. Do not make the observation seem weaker simply because its cause is unresolved. The combination still matters for prioritisation and protection.

Check whether a narrow operational step had broader business consequences. An endpoint restriction can affect a payments workflow while independent payment history remains unchanged. Verify the controlled path for essential work and record who will check it. A replacement device may restore capability without answering the security investigation. Keep the evidence for recovery and the assessment of data exposure as different tasks. The case lead can prioritise both without using one completed task to resolve the other automatically.

Then compare the scope note with actual source collection. The B30 proxy search is specific enough to support a bounded negative result. Endpoint health for another device and a missing host label indicate limitations that may require monitoring work, but they do not establish new impacted assets. A sound report says what additional evidence could update the count. For instance, a later valid record with an independently attributed device might justify a new pivot, while duplicate delivery should leave the actual observed scope unchanged. Ask whether the new evidence changes verified scope or simply prompts review. This is an important distinction when a case escalates across several teams.

Finally, give the incoming analyst a single next verification that will change a decision. If Finance can confirm a transaction change, state what response owner would need to be notified under the exercise. If mail supplies the original with trusted disposition data, state whether campaign recipients can be counted. If endpoint control status becomes uncertain, state which management record needs rechecking. A concise set of evidence-backed options is more usable than a general reminder to keep investigating.

## Key takeaways

- Preserve the difference between recorded events and the causal assessment.
- State the exact scope of controls, searches and outstanding impact.
- Keep request and effective-state evidence distinct throughout the chronology.
- Give each unresolved question an authorised owner and a verification method.
